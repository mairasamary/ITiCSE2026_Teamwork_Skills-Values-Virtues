# Focus Groups

Materials and data for the focus-group strand of WG10's educator-perspectives work (see [`../docs/project-overview.md`](../docs/project-overview.md) for how this fits into the group's overall methodology).

- [`protocol/`](./protocol/) — the focus-group script and preparation prompts (see below).
- `sessions/` — *placeholder, not yet populated.* Session notes, recordings metadata, or transcripts (de-identified as needed).
- `analysis/` — *placeholder, not yet populated.* Coding scheme and thematic analysis outputs.
- `README.md` (this file) — to be updated with a short description of who was recruited, how many sessions were run, and how findings feed into the working group's resources.

## Protocol

- [`protocol/focus-group-script-approved-pilot-notes.docx`](./protocol/focus-group-script-approved-pilot-notes.docx) — the approved moderator script: consent language, and the guiding questions and sub-questions asked during each session (team formation, structuring, scaffolding, assessment, and effectiveness), with pilot notes.
- [`protocol/focus-group-preparation-prompts.docx`](./protocol/focus-group-preparation-prompts.docx) / [`.pdf`](./protocol/focus-group-preparation-prompts.pdf) — the prompts sent to participants ahead of their session, including the working definitions of skills, values, and virtues used in the study.

**A note on participant data:** transcripts, recordings, and any personally identifiable information about participants should **not** be committed to this public GitHub repository. Store raw/identifiable data securely elsewhere (per your IRB protocol) and keep only de-identified, aggregated, or consented-for-sharing materials here.
