# Survey

Materials and data for the survey strand of WG10's educator-perspectives work (see [`../docs/project-overview.md`](../docs/project-overview.md) for how this fits into the group's overall methodology).

- [`instrument/`](./instrument/) — the survey questionnaire itself (see below).
- `data/` — not published here; see Participant Confidentiality below.
- `analysis/` — *placeholder, not yet populated.* Analysis scripts/notebooks and summary results.
- `codebook.md` — *placeholder, not yet populated.* A data dictionary describing each survey field/column.

## Instrument

[`instrument/pre-survey-v2.docx`](./instrument/pre-survey-v2.docx) is the pre-survey sent to computing educators, covering: academic teamwork (courses taught, enrollment, team counts), industry teamwork experience (optional), open-ended questions on what "teamwork" means and what tools are used to enact it, personal/demographic information (institution, years teaching, title, and optional gender identity and race/ethnicity), and a closing section inviting respondents to a follow-up focus group (see [`../focus-group/protocol/`](../focus-group/protocol/) for that protocol).

## Participant Confidentiality

Only the IRB-approved survey instrument above is published in this repository. Participants' answers, and their participation in the survey, are kept strictly confidential and are not shared here.
